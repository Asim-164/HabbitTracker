Help & Tips

How is the Health Score calculated?

The Health Score combines three factors for each habit over the last 7 days: Consistency (50%) — how many days you logged out of days you were expected to log. Accuracy (30%) — how close your actual values were to your targets. Streak (20%) — your current consecutive non-missed days. The final number is between 0 and 100.

How do streaks work?

A streak counts consecutive days where you logged something other than "Missed". A day with no log breaks the streak (treated the same as a missed day). Partial logs keep the streak alive. Only days from your habit's start date count.

What counts as a Missed day?

A day is "Missed" if you either logged it as Missed, or you didn't log it at all after the day passed. Days before a habit's start date and today (which is still in progress) are never counted as Missed.

How is the Weekly Review generated?

The Weekly Review shows last completed week (Monday–Sunday). It counts every expected day (logged or missed), identifies your Best Day (fewest misses) and Toughest Day (most misses), and highlights your strongest and weakest habits by completion rate.
